# Screening Inspection

- **Review:** First trimester serum tests for Down's syndrome screening
- **Review URL:** https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD011975/full
- **Screening criteria:** We included studies in which all women from a given population had one or more index test(s) compared to a reference standard (either chromosomal verification or macroscopic postnatal inspection). Both consecutive series and diagnostic case‐control study designs were included. Randomised trials where individuals were randomised to different screening strategies and all verified using a reference standard were also eligible for inclusion. Studies in which test strategies were compared head‐to‐head either in the same women, or between randomised groups were identified for inclusion in separate comparisons of test strategies. We excluded studies if they included less than five Down's syndrome cases, or more than 20% of participants were not followed up.
