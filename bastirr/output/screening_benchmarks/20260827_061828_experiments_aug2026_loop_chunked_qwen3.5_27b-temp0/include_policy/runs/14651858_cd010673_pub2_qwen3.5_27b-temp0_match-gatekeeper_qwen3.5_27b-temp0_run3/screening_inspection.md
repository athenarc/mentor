- **Screening criteria:** We considered all randomised controlled trials (RCTs), cluster‐randomised trials and cross‐over trials of psychological therapies for people (aged over 40 years) with COPD and coexisting anxiety disorders (as confirmed by recognised diagnostic criteria or a validated measurement scale), where this was compared with either no intervention or education only.
