- **Review:** Interventions for increasing fruit and vegetable consumption in children aged five years and under
- **Review URL:** https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD008552.pub7/full
- **Screening criteria:** We included randomised controlled trials, including cluster‐randomised controlled trials and cross‐over trials, of any intervention primarily targeting consumption of fruit, vegetables or both among children aged five years and under, and incorporating a dietary or biochemical assessment of fruit or vegetable consumption.
