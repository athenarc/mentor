# Excluded studies (title/abstract screening)

Records excluded during title/abstract screening (PRISMA 2020 item 16b).

| Study | Title | Reason |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 2 | Study 2 | Randomised trial but population is HIV-infected adults, not children <15 years or infants <18 months with HIV-infected mothers. Wrong population. |
| 3 | Study 3 | Randomised trial of cotrimoxazole prophylaxis, but population is adults (patients with Kaposi's sarcoma, typical adult AIDS presentation; adult dosing 800/160 mg twice daily; no mention of children). Does not meet age criteria. Wrong population. |

| 8 | Study 8 | Randomised controlled trial but in HIV-infected adults with tuberculosis (median age 32, range 18-64). Does not meet population criteria (children <15 or infants <18 months). |
| 9 | Study 9 | Review/editorial about mother-to-child transmission. Not a trial, no intervention. Not relevant study design. |
| 10 | Study 10 | Prospective observational study of PCP incidence in HIV-infected children. Not a randomised or quasi-randomised trial. Although some children were on prophylaxis, the study does not compare cotrimoxazole vs placebo/no treatment in a controlled design. |
