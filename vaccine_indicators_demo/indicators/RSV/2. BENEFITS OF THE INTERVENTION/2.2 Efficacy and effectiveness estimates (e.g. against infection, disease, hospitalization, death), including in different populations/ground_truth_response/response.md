# Efficacy and effectiveness estimates (e.g. against infection, disease, hospitalization, death), including in different populations

For maternal RSVPreF: Vaccine efficacy against RSV-positive severe medically-attended LRTI at 180 days after birth was 70 % (50.6, 82.5) and 49.2% (31.4, 62.8) against RSV-positive medically attended LRTI [1].

For nirsevimab: Efficacy against medically attended RSV-associated LRTI was 79% (68.5, 86.1). Efficacy against hospitalization for RSV-associated LRTI was 80.6% (62.3, 90.1) and efficacy against severe hospitalized RSV-associated LRTI was 78.5% (48.8,91.0) [2,3].

Both products have been shown to have high, similar effectiveness when used in real-world situations.
