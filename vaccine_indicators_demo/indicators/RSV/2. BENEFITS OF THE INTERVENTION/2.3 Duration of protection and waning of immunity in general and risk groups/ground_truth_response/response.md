# Duration of protection and waning of immunity in general and risk groups

The duration of protection is between 5-6 months. RSV antibody titers remained high 6 months after birth in infants born to mothers vaccinated with the maternal RSVPreF vaccine. Similarly, efficacy outcomes measured at 5 months after nirsevimab administration indicated protection at 5 months [1,2,3].
