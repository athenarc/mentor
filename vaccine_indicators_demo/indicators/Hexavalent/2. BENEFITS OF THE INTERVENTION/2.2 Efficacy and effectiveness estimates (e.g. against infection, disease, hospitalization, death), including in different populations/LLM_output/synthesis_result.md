**Pertussis (acellular pertussis component in hexavalent combinations, e.g., DT3aP-HBV-IPV/Hib; Pentaxim/Hexaxim aP antigens)**  
- In an efficacy trial setting using WHO-defined classical pertussis (≥21 days of paroxysmal cough), vaccine efficacy for the acellular pertussis (aP) antigens contained in Tetraxim/Pentaxim/Hexaxim was 85% (95% CI: 66–93) over a median follow-up of 1.58 years (range 0.01–4.25) [1].  
- In Sweden, real-world effectiveness of aP vaccines (including the aP antigens used in Pentaxim/Hexaxim) was sustained at 95–97% during 2007–2018 [1].  
- In Mexico, among infants <1 year hospitalized with confirmed pertussis (2011–2014; n=192), receipt of ≥2 aP doses was associated with fewer pertussis cases, and no pertussis cases were detected among infants who had received 3 doses [1].  
- In Mexico nationwide surveillance (2008–2019), three doses of Pentaxim or Hexaxim were associated with 97.2% protection against laboratory-confirmed pertussis, comparable to whole-cell pertussis vaccine protection (96.4%) [1].  
- In Switzerland, a nationwide study estimated vaccine effectiveness of DT3aP combination vaccines (pentavalent and hexavalent) against pertussis hospitalizations at 100% (95% CI: 97.9%–100%) following a 3+1 infant schedule [4].  
- Programmatic use of hexavalent DT3aP-HBV-IPV/Hib was associated with reductions in pertussis burden in observational analyses, including decreased pertussis-related hospitalization rates in Italy from 1.23 to 0.64 per 100,000 inhabitants between 2001 and 2014 (in a context where DT3aP-HBV-IPV/Hib was almost exclusively used since 2001 and coverage rose to >95% by 2011) [4], and a reported beneficial impact on pertussis incidence over 10 years in Australia [4].  
- Evidence from Sweden in the 1990s indicates that pertussis efficacy after three infant doses varied by aP antigen composition: 58.9% (95% CI: 50.9–65.9) for an experimental two-component aP vaccine and 85.2% (95% CI: 80.6–88.8) for an experimental five-component aP vaccine, using an endpoint of laboratory-confirmed pertussis or household contact with paroxysmal cough ≥21 days [2]. The same source notes a lack of published efficacy data specifically for DT2aP-IPV-Hib-HBV (a hexavalent vaccine containing 2 pertussis components) [2].

**Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib component in hexavalent combinations)**  
- In Germany, nationwide surveillance estimated effectiveness of hexavalent vaccines (DT3aP-HBV-IPV/Hib and Hexavac) against Hib at 90.4% after a 3-dose primary series and 100% after a toddler dose [4].  
- In the Netherlands, a case-control study estimated vaccine effectiveness of DT3aP-HBV-IPV/Hib against Hib at 94.0% (95% CI: 89.0%–96.8%), with evidence of waning with age [4].  
- Following implementation of hexavalent vaccination in Italy, Hib-related hospitalization rates declined from 5.4 to 2.4 per 100,000 among infants and from 2.3 to 0.9 per 100,000 among children aged 1–4 years between 2001 and 2005 [4].  
- Across countries introducing Hib conjugate vaccines in national immunization programmes, invasive Hib disease declined by >90% [8]. RCTs and observational studies have demonstrated protection against Hib meningitis, pneumonia, and other invasive Hib disease outcomes [8]. Reductions in nasopharyngeal Hib colonization after vaccine introduction have been observed, consistent with herd protection effects [8].  
- Cohort evidence suggests starting Hib vaccination at an older age may be associated with higher effectiveness against invasive Hib disease (no pooled numeric estimate reported) [8]. Sustained reductions in meningitis in young children have been reported after 5 years of Hib conjugate vaccine use with a 3p+0 schedule in several developing countries (no numeric estimate reported) [8]. In an evaluation from four South American countries, Hib meningitis rates 6–10 years post-introduction were reported as similar in countries using booster doses and those not using boosters (no numeric estimate reported) [8].

**Hepatitis B (HBV component in hexavalent combinations)**  
- In Australia, after implementation of DT3aP-HBV-IPV/Hib (2, 4, and 6 months), the overall incidence of HBV decreased from 33.1 to 24.2 cases per 100,000 population between 2009 and 2018 in an observational analysis [4].

**Other hexavalent components (diphtheria, tetanus, poliovirus)**  
- No efficacy/effectiveness estimates against diphtheria, tetanus, or poliomyelitis outcomes were provided in the extracted evidence [1, 4, 8, 2].
